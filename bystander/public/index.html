Is the person unresponsive and not breathing normally?

Tap their shoulders and shout. Watch the chest for up to 10 seconds. Gasping is not normal breathing.

Roll them onto their side (recovery position), keep their airway open, and stay with them. Call 911. If they stop breathing normally, start CPR.
